## 🚀 Key Features
1. **Editorial UI/UX Design**: Built with custom typography (`Syne`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `JetBrains Mono`), glassmorphism, and responsive Bento grids.
2. **Flagship Research Case Study**: Interactive explorer for *"Corporate Life of Gen Z"* analyzing workplace autonomy, tax-saving behaviors, and retention metrics.
3. **Financial Competencies Matrix**: Direct & Indirect Taxation, GST compliance, financial modeling, auditing standards, and corporate reporting.
4. **The Cinephile Vault**: Exploration of cinema analysis and narrative framing as an analytical business advantage.
5. **Interactive Elements**: Magnetic buttons, 3D tilt card physics, modal abstract reader, live Kochi IST clock, and one-click email copy.
